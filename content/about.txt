I'm a PhD candidate in Energy-Meteorology at the University of Cologne, investigating how machine-learning weather prediction (MLWP) models can forecast renewable power generation. My work sits at the intersection of atmospheric science, data engineering, and machine learning.

Before this, I built a global database of extreme weather events from CMIP6 climate model data, studied atmospheric chemistry at Forschungszentrum Jülich, and spent a year and a half writing scripts for a science YouTube channel. I started out as a paramedic and worked as a nurse, in geriatric care and later an intensive care unit, before going to university to study physics.
